# Acme Corp — Company Leave & Vacation Policy (2026)
**Document Code:** POL-HR-2026-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applies To:** All Full-Time & Part-Time Employees  

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy outlines the guidelines, entitlement rules, and approval procedures for annual leave, sick leave, parental leave, and bereavement leave at Acme Corp.

---

## 2. Annual Paid Time Off (PTO)
- **Accrual Rate:** Full-time employees accrue **20 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year (1.67 days per month).
- **Rollover:** A maximum of **5 unused leave days** may be carried over into the following calendar year. Any additional unused days expire on March 31st.
- **Advance Notice:**
  - Leaves lasting **1 to 2 consecutive days** require at least **24 hours advance notice** in the HR portal.
  - Leaves lasting **3 to 5 consecutive days** require at least **1 week advance notice** and manager approval.
  - Leaves exceeding **5 consecutive working days** require at least **3 weeks advance notice** and department head approval.

---

## 3. Sick Leave & Medical Emergencies
- Full-time employees receive **10 days of paid sick leave** annually.
- A registered medical practitioner's certificate is mandatory for consecutive sick absences of **3 days or longer**.
- Mental wellness days: Employees may utilize up to **2 days of their sick leave balance** per quarter as proactive mental wellness days without a doctor's note.

---

## 4. Parental & Caregiver Leave
- **Primary Caregiver (Maternity/Adoption):** **16 weeks of fully paid leave**.
- **Secondary Caregiver (Paternity/Partner):** **4 weeks of fully paid leave**.
- Parental leave must be commenced within the first 12 months following the child's birth or official adoption placement.

---

## 5. Public Holidays & Floating Holidays
Acme Corp observes **10 statutory public holidays** and provides **2 floating religious/cultural holidays** per year that employees can schedule at their discretion.

---

## 6. Approvals & Submission Process
All leave requests must be submitted through the internal portal (**KnowFlow Portal ➔ Time Off**). Unauthorized absences exceeding 3 consecutive working days without contact will be considered job abandonment.
