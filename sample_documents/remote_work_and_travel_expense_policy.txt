ACME CORP - REMOTE WORK & TRAVEL EXPENSE POLICY
Document ID: POL-FIN-2026-04
Classification: Internal / Confidential

1. WORK FROM HOME & REMOTE ELIGIBILITY
Employees in hybrid or fully remote roles are eligible for a one-time home office equipment stipend of $500 upon hire to cover ergonomic chairs, secondary monitors, or desk setups.
Monthly Internet reimbursement of up to $60 is claimable through Expensify with attached monthly billing statements.

2. DOMESTIC & INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS TRAVEL
- Air Travel: Economy class is standard for domestic flights under 5 hours. Business class is permitted for continuous international flights exceeding 6 hours with VP approval.
- Lodging: Preferred corporate hotel network maximum allowance is $200/night in standard metros, and $300/night in Tier-1 cities (NYC, SF, London, Tokyo).
- Per Diem Meals: Daily meal allowance is $75 per travel day ($20 breakfast, $25 lunch, $30 dinner). Alcohol is not reimbursable unless as part of an approved client entertainment dinner.

3. EXPENSE REPORTING & RECEIPT SUBMISSION
All expense reports must be submitted within 14 calendar days of returning from travel. Itemized receipts are mandatory for any expense exceeding $25. Credit card summary slips without itemized breakdown will be rejected.
Approval SLA for travel reimbursement is 5 business days following manager sign-off.
